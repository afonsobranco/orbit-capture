How to use

Orbit Capture · Quick Reference

1
Choose your format
WebM for GitHub PRs and docs — smaller, sharper, better quality. GIF for maximum compatibility. Audio capture is only available in WebM and must be enabled before recording starts.
2
Hit record
Click the crimson button. Your browser will ask which screen, window, or tab to capture. A 3-second countdown gives you time to arrange your view before recording begins.
3
Stop and trim
Click again to stop. Drag the blue IN handle and red OUT handle on the timeline to define your clip. Click any thumbnail frame to scrub to that moment. The estimated file size updates live.
4
Export and download
Hit Export ↓ to encode and download your clip. GIF clips over 30 s trigger a warning — expect files above 20 MB. WebM exports stream in real time through a canvas re-encode.
5
Install as a desktop app
Chrome and Edge show an install prompt (⊕) in the address bar. Click it to run Orbit Capture as a standalone app with no browser chrome, working offline for WebM recordings.

Everything happens locally in your browser. No data is uploaded or stored anywhere.
